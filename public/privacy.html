6. Your Rights

7. Calendar Integrations

If you connect your Google Calendar or Calendly account in Settings → Integrations, Agently accesses that account solely to book and manage appointments during phone calls handled by your AI voice agents. Connecting is optional, and Agently never accesses a calendar you have not explicitly connected.

What we access from Google, and why:

  • Free/busy information (calendar.freebusy) — to check which time slots are open before the agent offers them to a caller. We do not read the titles, attendees or details of your existing events for this purpose, only whether a time range is free or busy.
  • Calendar events (calendar.events) — to create an event when a caller books, and to cancel it if the booking is cancelled. Agently only creates, updates or deletes events it created itself, or that are directly tied to a booking made through Agently. It does not modify your other events.
  • Basic profile (openid, email, profile) — to show which Google account is connected, so you can confirm the right calendar is linked.

From Calendly, we request only what booking requires: reading your user profile, event types and availability, creating and cancelling scheduled events, and subscribing to webhooks so we learn about changes made outside Agently.

Agently's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use calendar data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not allow humans to read it except where required for security, to comply with the law, or with your explicit consent.

What we store. Your calendar credentials are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM and are bound to your organisation, so a stored credential cannot be read in another tenant's context. For each appointment booked through Agently we also store the booking details supplied by the caller — typically a name, email address and phone number — together with the appointment time, so the booking can be shown to you, reconciled with your calendar and cancelled if needed.

Disconnecting. You can disconnect at any time in Settings → Integrations. Doing so revokes Agently's access at the provider and deletes the stored credentials. You can also revoke access directly at myaccount.google.com/permissions for Google, or in your Calendly account settings.
